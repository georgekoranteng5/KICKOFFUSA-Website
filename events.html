FAQs

Everything you need to know before you register

Do I need a team to register, or can I sign up as a free agent?

You need to register with a team unless a specific event is advertised as accepting free agents. Check the event details or reach out if you're unsure.

Do I need astros or grass boots?

Either is fine. Wear what you're comfortable playing in for the surface at the venue.

How do I join a team if I don't have one?

Contact us and we'll do our best to connect you with a registered captain who has open spots.

Can I register on game day?

No. All registration must be completed at least 7 days before the event.
