Our Mission

KICKOFFUSA is an Atlanta-based soccer initiative united by culture and community. We believe the game doesn't end at the final whistle. It lives on in how we connect, compete, and show up for each other on and off the pitch.

Driven by culture with the aim of building a community rooted in soccer for neighbors, families, and fans who love the beautiful game.
