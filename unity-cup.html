The Tournament

Welcome to the inaugural KICKOFF UNITY CUP, a citywide 5v5 charity soccer tournament that brings together Atlanta's leading Black entertainment groups for a day of competition, community, and impact.

This event is about uniting influential social communities through a shared experience that goes beyond nightlife and into meaningful engagement. It's an opportunity to showcase your brand, connect with other groups, and be part of the larger Atlanta social scene.

Atlanta Children's Day Shelter

Atlanta Children's Day Shelter provides free, high-quality childcare to families experiencing extreme poverty or homelessness. They are a day shelter open from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, where families enroll for long-term assistance.

Students are under the age of 6 and are divided into five age groups: infants, toddler 1, toddler 2, and preschool. The shelter also provides comprehensive Family Services to parents, including education and employment opportunities, mental health services, and financial assistance.
